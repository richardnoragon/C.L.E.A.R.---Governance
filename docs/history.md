# Governance History

This document records major milestones in the evolution of the C.L.E.A.R.
governance system. It serves as the institutional memory of the framework.

---

## Origins

The C.L.E.A.R. governance model was created to address recurring issues in
cross‑team alignment, inconsistent standards, and governance drift.  
Its purpose is to provide a deterministic, transparent, and role‑anchored
approach to standardization.

---

## Foundational Milestones

### 1. Governance Charter Established
The constitutional foundation defining roles, responsibilities, and lifecycle
principles.

### 2. Lifecycle Specification Approved
The five‑stage lifecycle (Codify → Legitimize → Enforce → Adopt → Review) was
formalized and adopted.

### 3. Role Definitions Published
Steward, Author, Reviewer, Enforcement Engineer, and Team Lead roles were
codified.

### 4. Enforcement Framework Introduced
CI, linters, scaffolds, and reference implementations became mandatory for all
standards.

### 5. Review Cycle System Implemented
Scheduled reviews and archival processes were established to prevent governance
drift.

## Future Directions

- Expansion of cross‑team working groups  
- Automated governance validation tooling  
- Enhanced metrics and reporting  
- Broader adoption across additional domains  

The governance system is designed to evolve responsibly through evidence and
review.
