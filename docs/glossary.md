# Glossary

This glossary defines key terms used throughout the C.L.E.A.R. governance system.
All definitions are normative unless otherwise stated.

---

## Adoption
The process by which teams implement an approved and enforced standard.

## ADR (Architecture Decision Record)
A structured document capturing a significant architectural decision, its context,
rationale, and consequences.

## Anti‑Pattern
A recurring behavior or structure that undermines governance quality or lifecycle
integrity.

## Author
The role responsible for drafting a standard during the Codify stage.

## Codify
The lifecycle stage where intent becomes a rationale‑driven, reviewable
specification.

## Decision Record
A documented outcome of the Legitimize stage, including rationale and required
changes.

## Enforce
The lifecycle stage where standards become mechanically enforced through CI,
linters, scaffolds, and reference implementations.

## Enforcement Engineer
The role responsible for implementing deterministic enforcement mechanisms.

## Governance Steward
The custodian of the lifecycle and guardian of governance integrity.

## Legitimize
The lifecycle stage where proposals undergo structured, evidence‑based review.

## Migration Guide
A document describing how teams transition to a new standard.

## Normative Language
Formal language used in specifications:
- MUST / MUST NOT  
- SHOULD / SHOULD NOT  
- MAY  

## Review
The lifecycle stage where standards are evaluated for continued relevance and
effectiveness.

## Rollout Plan
A coordinated plan describing how a standard will be introduced across teams and
systems.

## Standard
A rule, pattern, or requirement that has passed through all lifecycle stages and
is enforceable and adoptable.

## Steward Verification
The Steward’s confirmation that a lifecycle stage has been completed correctly.

## Traceability
The ability to follow a standard’s history across all lifecycle stages.
