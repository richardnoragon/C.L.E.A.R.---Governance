# Governance Steward

The Governance Steward is the custodian of the C.L.E.A.R. lifecycle and the guarantor of its integrity.  
The Steward ensures that governance is practiced consistently, transparently, and in accordance with the constitutional charter.

The Steward does not own standards — the Steward owns the **process** that governs them.

---

## Purpose

- Safeguard the lifecycle and ensure no stage is skipped
- Maintain clarity, traceability, and accountability across all governance work
- Protect the system from ambiguity, drift, and procedural shortcuts

---

## Responsibilities

- Validate that proposals are ready to enter Legitimize
- Facilitate structured reviews and prevent scope creep
- Ensure all required artifacts are complete and traceable
- Verify that enforcement matches the approved specification
- Monitor adoption and escalate systemic blockers
- Initiate scheduled reviews and manage review cycles
- Maintain governance documentation and templates

---

## Authority

- Reject proposals that violate lifecycle requirements
- Require revisions, clarifications, or additional evidence
- Mandate enforcement mechanisms before adoption
- Pause or roll back standards that cause systemic issues
- Retire obsolete standards through the Review process

---

## Required Practices

- Uphold neutrality and process discipline
- Document all decisions and rationale
- Ensure transparency across teams and systems
- Maintain alignment with the governance charter

---

## Anti‑Patterns

- Allowing proposals to skip lifecycle stages
- Silent approvals or undocumented decisions
- Overstepping into authorship or reviewer roles
- Allowing governance drift due to inaction
