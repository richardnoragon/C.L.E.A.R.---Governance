# Enforcement Engineer

The Enforcement Engineer ensures that approved standards become lived practice through deterministic automation.  
They translate governance intent into CI rules, linters, scaffolds, and reference implementations.

The Enforcement Engineer does not interpret standards — they **mechanize** them.

---

## Purpose

- Implement enforcement mechanisms that are objective, reproducible, and aligned with the specification
- Ensure standards are applied consistently across teams and systems

---

## Responsibilities

- Design enforcement strategies based on the approved specification
- Implement CI rules, linter configurations, and scaffolds
- Create and maintain reference implementations
- Validate enforcement correctness and determinism
- Document enforcement behavior and configuration
- Support teams during adoption

---

## Authority

- Define the technical shape of enforcement
- Reject enforcement requests that contradict the specification
- Require revisions to specifications that cannot be enforced mechanically

---

## Required Practices

- Ensure enforcement is deterministic and reproducible
- Avoid over‑enforcement or under‑enforcement
- Maintain alignment with the Steward and Author
- Keep enforcement mechanisms up to date as standards evolve

---

## Anti‑Patterns

- Manual enforcement (“just remind people”)
- Enforcement that contradicts the specification
- Overly strict rules that block legitimate use cases
- Undocumented enforcement behavior
