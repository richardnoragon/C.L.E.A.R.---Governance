# Reviewer

The Reviewer is the guardian of quality, compatibility, and long‑term maintainability.  
Reviewers ensure that standards are correct, coherent, enforceable, and aligned with the broader ecosystem.

Reviewers do not rewrite proposals — they evaluate them.

---

## Purpose

- Validate the proposal through structured, evidence‑based review
- Protect the system from unclear, conflicting, or unenforceable standards
- Ensure long‑term maintainability and cross‑team compatibility

---

## Responsibilities

- Evaluate proposals using the Reviewer Checklist
- Identify risks, conflicts, and missing rationale
- Ensure the proposal is complete, coherent, and enforceable
- Provide clear, actionable, evidence‑based feedback
- Record decisions and rationale transparently

---

## Authority

- Approve, request changes, reject, or table proposals
- Require additional analysis or evidence
- Escalate governance concerns to the Steward

---

## Required Practices

- Maintain objectivity and independence
- Focus on correctness, clarity, and enforceability
- Avoid personal preference or stylistic debates
- Ensure decisions are documented and justified

---

## Anti‑Patterns

- Rubber‑stamping
- Bikeshedding
- Reviewer overreach (rewriting the proposal)
- Silent approval or undocumented decisions
