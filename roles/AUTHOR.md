# Author

The Author transforms intent into a codified, rationale‑driven, reviewable specification.  
The Author is responsible for the clarity, completeness, and correctness of the proposal.

The Author does not own the decision — the Author owns the **draft**.

---

## Purpose

- Capture the problem, rationale, and proposed solution
- Produce a clear, enforceable, review‑ready specification
- Ensure the proposal reflects stakeholder needs and constraints

---

## Responsibilities

- Write the initial draft specification
- Document goals, non‑goals, rationale, and alternatives
- Conduct impact analysis and identify affected teams
- Collaborate with SMEs for correctness and feasibility
- Revise the proposal based on reviewer feedback
- Support enforcement and adoption with domain expertise

---

## Authority

- Define the initial shape of the standard
- Clarify intent and rationale during review
- Propose revisions during scheduled reviews

---

## Required Practices

- Use normative language (MUST, SHOULD, MUST NOT)
- Ensure the proposal is enforceable in principle
- Provide complete, transparent rationale
- Avoid hidden constraints or tribal knowledge

---

## Anti‑Patterns

- Writing vague or unenforceable standards
- Omitting rationale or alternatives
- Over‑scoping or under‑scoping the proposal
- Treating review feedback as optional
