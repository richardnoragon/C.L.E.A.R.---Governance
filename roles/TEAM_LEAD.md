# Team Lead

The Team Lead is accountable for adoption within their domain.  
They ensure that their team understands, implements, and lives the approved standards.

The Team Lead does not approve standards — they **operationalize** them.

---

## Purpose

- Ensure successful adoption of standards across their team
- Provide real‑world feedback during review cycles
- Surface blockers and risks early

---

## Responsibilities

- Communicate standards to their team
- Plan and execute migrations according to rollout timelines
- Report adoption metrics and blockers
- Participate in scheduled reviews with real‑world insights
- Ensure team practices remain aligned with governance

---

## Authority

- Request clarifications or revisions when standards impede delivery
- Propose exceptions with documented rationale
- Prioritize adoption work within team planning

---

## Required Practices

- Maintain transparency with the Steward
- Ensure team members understand expectations
- Track and report adoption progress
- Escalate systemic blockers promptly

---

## Anti‑Patterns

- Assuming adoption will “just happen”
- Failing to communicate standards to the team
- Ignoring blockers or failing to report them
- Allowing team‑local deviations without governance approval
