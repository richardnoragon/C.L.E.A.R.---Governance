# Adopt Stage

The Adopt stage governs how standards move from approval into **real‑world usage**.  
A standard is not successful when it is approved — it is successful when it is lived.

Adoption is where governance meets reality.

---

## Purpose

- Ensure teams understand and implement the standard
- Provide clear migration paths and timelines
- Measure adoption progress and identify blockers
- Support teams through transition

Adoption transforms enforcement from a mechanism into a practice.

---

## Scope

Adopt applies to:

- Rollout planning
- Migration guides
- Communication and onboarding
- Adoption metrics and reporting
- Team‑level implementation

If a standard affects teams, it must have an adoption plan.

---

# Sub‑Stages

## A1. Rollout Planning

- Define scope and timeline
- Identify affected teams and systems
- Determine migration sequencing
- Establish communication channels

Outputs:
- Rollout Plan
- Communication Plan

---

## A2. Communication

- Announce the standard and its rationale
- Provide documentation and examples
- Offer Q&A sessions or office hours
- Ensure teams understand expectations

Outputs:
- Announcement
- Contributor‑friendly documentation

---

## A3. Migration

- Teams implement the standard
- Migration guides provide step‑by‑step instructions
- Blockers are tracked and escalated
- Steward monitors progress

Outputs:
- Migration progress reports
- Blocker logs

---

## A4. Measurement

- Track adoption metrics
- Identify lagging areas
- Adjust rollout plans as needed
- Prepare for Review stage

Outputs:
- Adoption Metrics
- Adoption Summary

---

# Required Artifacts

- Migration Guide  
- Rollout Plan  
- Adoption Metrics  
- Contributor Documentation  
- Blocker Log (if needed)  

---

# Roles and Responsibilities

## Team Leads (Lead)

- Ensure adoption within their domain
- Report progress and blockers
- Coordinate migration work

## Governance Steward (Monitor)

- Tracks adoption across teams
- Escalates systemic blockers
- Ensures transparency and accountability

## Author (Support)

- Provides clarifications
- Assists with migration guidance

---

# Entry and Exit Criteria

## Entry Criteria

- Enforcement mechanisms complete
- Migration path defined
- Documentation published

## Exit Criteria

- Adoption metrics collected
- Migration completed or in progress according to plan
- Steward confirms readiness for Review

---

# Quality Bar

Adoption is complete when:

- Teams understand the standard
- Migration is underway or complete
- Blockers are documented and addressed
- Metrics show meaningful progress

---

# Anti‑Patterns

- “It’s approved, so everyone will just use it”
- No migration path
- No communication plan
- No measurement
- Breaking changes without support

---

# Checklist for “Adopt Complete”

- [ ] Migration guide published  
- [ ] Rollout plan executed  
- [ ] Teams onboarded  
- [ ] Adoption metrics collected  
- [ ] Steward confirms readiness for Review
