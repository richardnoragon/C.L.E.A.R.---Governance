# Review Stage

The Review stage ensures that standards remain **relevant, effective, and aligned** with evolving needs.  
No standard is permanent.  
Review is the mechanism that keeps governance alive.

---

## Purpose

- Evaluate whether the standard still serves its purpose
- Identify pain points, exceptions, and drift
- Incorporate evidence from real‑world usage
- Decide whether to reaffirm, revise, replace, or retire the standard

Review prevents stagnation and governance decay.

---

## Scope

Review applies to:

- All approved standards
- All enforced rules
- All adopted practices
- Any standard that has reached its scheduled review date

Every standard must be reviewed on a predictable cadence.

---

# Sub‑Stages

## R1. Scheduled Review

- Triggered by the review schedule
- Steward initiates the review process
- Teams and SMEs are notified

Outputs:
- Review kickoff

---

## R2. Evidence Gathering

- Collect adoption metrics
- Gather feedback from teams
- Identify exceptions and deviations
- Analyze enforcement logs
- Review architectural or UX impacts

Outputs:
- Evidence Summary

---

## R3. Decision

Possible outcomes:

- **Reaffirm** — Standard remains valid  
- **Revise** — Standard requires updates  
- **Replace** — Standard is superseded  
- **Retire** — Standard is deprecated  

Every decision must include rationale.

Outputs:
- Review Report
- Revision or Retirement Proposal (if applicable)

---

# Required Artifacts

- Review Report  
- Evidence Summary  
- Revision Proposal (if needed)  
- Deprecation Notice (if retiring)  

---

# Roles and Responsibilities

## Governance Steward (Lead)

- Initiates and facilitates the review
- Ensures evidence is collected
- Records decisions and rationale

## Domain SMEs (Support)

- Provide technical, UX, or process insight
- Identify emerging needs or conflicts

## Teams (Feedback Providers)

- Report real‑world experience
- Highlight pain points and exceptions

---

# Entry and Exit Criteria

## Entry Criteria

- Scheduled review date reached
- Adoption metrics available
- Enforcement logs available

## Exit Criteria

- Decision recorded with rationale
- Revision or retirement plan created (if applicable)
- Standard reaffirmed or updated

---

# Quality Bar

A review is complete when:

- Evidence is comprehensive and representative
- Decision is justified and documented
- Next steps are clear and actionable
- The standard is aligned with current needs

---

# Anti‑Patterns

- Reviews that are symbolic only
- No evidence collected
- Decisions based on intuition, not data
- Standards that never change
- “We’ll fix it next cycle”

---

# Checklist for “Review Complete”

- [ ] Evidence gathered  
- [ ] Review report written  
- [ ] Decision recorded  
- [ ] Revision or retirement plan created (if needed)  
- [ ] Steward closes the review cycle
