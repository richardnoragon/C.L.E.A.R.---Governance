# C.L.E.A.R. Governance System (Repository Ovverview)

The C.L.E.A.R. Governance System defines a deterministic, transparent, and role‑anchored lifecycle for creating, validating, enforcing, adopting, and reviewing standards across teams and systems.

This repository contains:

- The constitutional **CHARTER.md**  
- The formal lifecycle **SPEC.md**  
- Role definitions under `roles/`  
- Stage definitions under `lifecycle/`  
- Templates for standards, reviews, ADRs, and migrations  
- Anti‑patterns to avoid governance drift  
- Enforcement, adoption, and review cycle materials  
- Supporting documentation under `docs/`  

## Purpose

C.L.E.A.R. exists to ensure that governance is:

- **Codified** — Intent becomes explicit, rationale‑driven specification  
- **Legitimized** — Standards are validated through structured review  
- **Enforced** — Rules are applied mechanically, not socially  
- **Adopted** — Teams migrate with guidance and measurable progress  
- **Reviewed** — Standards evolve through evidence and reflection  

## Philosophy

Governance is not a document.  
Governance is a lived practice upheld through roles, responsibilities, and transparency.

This repository provides the structure, artifacts, and processes required to maintain that practice.

## Getting Started

1. Read the **CHARTER.md** to understand the constitutional foundation.  
2. Review the **SPEC.md** to learn the lifecycle.  
3. Explore `roles/` to understand responsibilities.  
4. Use templates in `templates/` to propose new standards.  
5. Follow the lifecycle defined in `lifecycle/` for all governance work.  

## Contributing

All contributions must follow the C.L.E.A.R. lifecycle.  
No standard, rule, or pattern is valid unless it has passed through all five stages.
