# Review Anti‑Patterns

Review anti‑patterns undermine the legitimacy, quality, and transparency of the governance process.  
This document identifies common failures in the Legitimize stage and how to avoid them.

---

## 1. Rubber‑Stamping
Approving without deep evaluation.

**Why it’s harmful:**  
Allows unclear or broken standards into the system.

**Prevention:**  
Use the Reviewer Checklist rigorously.

---

## 2. Bikeshedding
Debating trivial details instead of core issues.

**Why it’s harmful:**  
Wastes time and delays progress.

**Prevention:**  
Focus on correctness, clarity, enforceability.

---

## 3. Reviewer Overreach
Reviewers rewriting the proposal instead of evaluating it.

**Why it’s harmful:**  
Blurs roles and undermines the Author.

**Prevention:**  
Reviewers evaluate; Authors write.

---

## 4. Unbounded Review
Endless feedback loops with no closure.

**Why it’s harmful:**  
Stalls governance and increases debt.

**Prevention:**  
Steward enforces review discipline.

---

## 5. Missing Rationale
Decision recorded without explanation.

**Why it’s harmful:**  
Destroys traceability and institutional memory.

**Prevention:**  
Every decision must include rationale.

---

## 6. Ignoring Enforceability
Approving rules that cannot be automated.

**Why it’s harmful:**  
Creates standards that cannot be lived.

**Prevention:**  
Enforceability is a core review criterion.

---

## 7. Ignoring Adoption
Approving rules that teams cannot realistically implement.

**Why it’s harmful:**  
Creates governance that exists only on paper.

**Prevention:**  
Review must consider migration feasibility.

---

## 8. Conflicts with Existing Standards
Failing to check compatibility.

**Why it’s harmful:**  
Creates contradictions and confusion.

**Prevention:**  
Reviewers must cross‑reference the standards library.

---

## 9. Lack of Independence
Reviewers from the same team as the Author.

**Why it’s harmful:**  
Introduces bias and weakens legitimacy.

**Prevention:**  
Reviewers must be independent.

---

## 10. No Steward Oversight
Review proceeds without Steward facilitation.

**Why it’s harmful:**  
Lifecycle discipline breaks down.

**Prevention:**  
Steward must validate every review.
