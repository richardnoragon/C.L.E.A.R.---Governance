# Review Archive

This directory stores all completed Review Reports.  
Each report provides a transparent, traceable record of how standards evolve over
time.

The archive is the institutional memory of the governance system.

---

## Purpose

- Preserve historical decisions and rationale  
- Provide evidence for future revisions  
- Enable traceability across lifecycle stages  
- Support audits and compliance reviews  

---

## Structure

Each review report should be stored as:

<STANDARD_ID>-review-YYYY-MM-DD.md
Example:
----
STD-001-review-2026-05-12.md

---

## Steward Responsibilities

- Ensure every completed review is archived  
- Maintain consistent naming conventions  
- Ensure reports are complete and readable  
- Link archived reports from the standard’s changelog  

---

## Anti‑Patterns

- Missing or incomplete review reports  
- Reports stored outside the archive  
- Decisions without rationale  
- Untraceable revisions  

---

## Success Criteria

The archive is healthy when:

- Every standard has a complete review history  
- Reports are easy to navigate and understand  
- Decisions are traceable across lifecycle stages
