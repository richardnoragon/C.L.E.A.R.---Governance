# Review Schedule

Every standard in the C.L.E.A.R. governance system must undergo a scheduled review
to ensure it remains relevant, effective, and aligned with evolving needs.

Review is not optional — it is the mechanism that prevents governance drift.

---

## Review Cadence

Each standard MUST define its own review interval during Codify.  
If not explicitly defined, the default cadence applies:

- **Annual Review (every 12 months)** for all active standards  
- **Semi‑Annual Review (every 6 months)** for high‑impact or high‑risk standards  
- **Quarterly Review (every 3 months)** for experimental or rapidly evolving standards  

---

## Review Triggers

A review may also be triggered outside the schedule when:

- Enforcement logs show recurring violations  
- Adoption metrics indicate systemic blockers  
- Architectural or UX changes invalidate assumptions  
- A new standard supersedes or conflicts with an existing one  
- Teams request reconsideration due to emerging constraints  

---

## Responsibilities

### Steward
- Initiates scheduled reviews  
- Coordinates evidence gathering  
- Ensures review artifacts are complete  
- Records decisions and rationale  

### Domain SMEs
- Provide technical, UX, or process insights  
- Identify emerging needs or conflicts  

### Teams
- Provide real‑world feedback  
- Report blockers, exceptions, and drift
