# Rollout Plans

Rollout Plans define how a standard is introduced across teams, systems, and
repositories. They ensure that adoption is coordinated, predictable, and
transparent.

A Rollout Plan is required for every standard that impacts multiple teams or
systems.

---

## Purpose

- Define scope, timeline, and sequencing  
- Identify affected teams and systems  
- Establish communication and support channels  
- Provide a roadmap for adoption  

---

## What Belongs in This Directory

- Rollout plans for individual standards  
- Cross‑team coordination documents  
- Communication plans  
- Risk assessments and mitigation strategies  

---

## Principles

- Rollout MUST be planned before adoption begins  
- Rollout MUST be transparent and communicated early  
- Rollout SHOULD minimize disruption to delivery  
- Rollout SHOULD include checkpoints and review gates  

---

## Steward Verification

The Steward ensures that:

- Rollout plans align with migration guides  
- Timelines are realistic and measurable  
- Risks and dependencies are documented
