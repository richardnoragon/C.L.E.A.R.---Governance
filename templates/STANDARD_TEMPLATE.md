# Standard Proposal Template

## Status
Draft | In Review | Approved | Enforced | Adopted | Under Review | Deprecated

## Lifecycle Stage
Codify → Legitimize → Enforce → Adopt → Review

---

# 1. Problem Statement
Describe the concrete problem this standard solves.  
Avoid solutions, opinions, or assumptions.

---

# 2. Goals
List the intended outcomes of this standard.

---

# 3. Non‑Goals
List what this standard explicitly does NOT aim to solve.

---

# 4. Rationale
Explain *why this standard is needed* and *why this approach*.  
Include evidence, constraints, and motivating examples.

---

# 5. Specification (Normative)
Define the rule using normative language:

- MUST / MUST NOT  
- SHOULD / SHOULD NOT  
- MAY  

This section becomes the enforceable source of truth.

---

# 6. Alternatives Considered
Document rejected options and why they were not chosen.

---

# 7. Impact Analysis
Describe the blast radius:

- Affected teams  
- Affected systems  
- Architectural implications  
- UX, security, compliance considerations  

---

# 8. Migration Plan (Initial)
High‑level steps for teams to adopt the standard.

---

# 9. Enforcement Strategy (Initial)
Describe how this standard *could* be enforced mechanically.

---

# 10. Review Cycle
Define how often this standard must be reviewed (e.g., every 12 months).

---

# 11. Changelog
Track revisions to this standard.
